Core Pillars of Safety & AI Risk Analytics

1. Evidence-Based Crime Rate Normalization

Every risk metric is population-adjusted per 100,000 residents based on official Census of India enumeration (2001, 2011) and projected demographics up to 2026. Avoids raw count bias across small vs metropolis regions.

2. Leakage-Safe Deep Learning Neural Networks

Feed-forward neural network architecture featuring Dense, Batch Normalization, and Dropout layers. Features for year T use strictly shifted historical data (T-1) to ensure zero future-data leakage.

3. Transparent Risk Tier Classification

Cities and districts are binned into LOW 🟢, MEDIUM 🟡, and HIGH 🔴 historical risk tiers using cross-sectional composite percentile ranks of crime rate level and trailing trend trajectory.

4. 2001–2026 Predictive Trajectory Forecasting

Provides research-grade forecasts through 2026 for 182 districts across India, helping public policy researchers, citizens, and safety analysts examine long-term statistical trends.

Official Data Standard & Safety Context
This platform measures reported historical crime data & statistical projections. Reported crime statistics reflect police reporting practices, population density, policing intensity, and societal reporting behavior — not a literal measure of individual safety or guaranteed future events. All data is derived from NCRB open records and official Census projections.
